Good Questions, Plain Answers

Frequently asked.

How far in advance should we book?

Spring and fall Saturdays go first, often 8-12 months out. Off-season and weekday dates offer more flexibility and friendlier rates.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Yes. The kitchen area is ready for your caterer's team, and we'll coordinate access and timing with them directly.

Do you provide tables, chairs and set-up?

Yes. Tables and chairs are included with every rental, and the floor plan is laid out with you before event day.

What about music, microphones and presentations?

The hall is A/V-ready with a stage and house sound for toasts, music and slideshows. Larger productions can bring in outside A/V.

Is there a deposit, and when is the balance due?

A signed agreement and deposit reserve your date. The payment schedule is explained clearly up front — no surprises.
