Good Questions, Plain Answers

Frequently asked.

How do we get tickets to a show?

Call or text 214-699-9868 for showtimes, tickets and season information. Current productions are listed right on this page at launch.

How do I audition for a production?

The venue holds open auditions for local performers. Call to find out the next audition date and what to prepare.

Can our theater group rent the stage?

Yes. The Stage Rental package covers rehearsal and performance blocks with theatrical lighting, house sound and seating configuration.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Yes. The kitchen area is ready for your caterer's team, and we'll coordinate access and timing with them directly.

Do you provide tables, chairs and set-up?

Yes. Tables and chairs are included with every rental, and the floor plan is laid out with you before event day.

Is there a deposit, and when is the balance due?

A signed agreement and deposit reserve your date. The payment schedule is explained clearly up front — no surprises.
